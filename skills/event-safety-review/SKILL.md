# Event Safety Review

## Purpose
Identify safety-related planning considerations for an event and provide recommendations for human review.

## Inputs
- Event plan
- Venue capacity
- Available safety and accessibility information

## Review Areas
- Venue capacity and occupancy limits
- Emergency exits and evacuation arrangements
- Fire safety provisions
- Accessibility requirements
- Staffing and emergency contacts

## Rules
- Use only supplied information.
- Clearly identify missing or unverified safety information.
- Do not claim that a venue is legally compliant or safe without verified evidence.
- Do not replace qualified safety personnel or official inspections.
- Recommend that the event organizer obtain the required approvals before the event.
